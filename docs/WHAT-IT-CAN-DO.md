# What to say to it

Say **"hey jarvis"**, wait for `[listening]`, then talk normally. It stops
recording when you stop talking — there is no button to hold and no need to
rush.

Say **"stop"** to cut it off mid-sentence. Say **"goodbye"** to shut it down.

---

## Time and date

> hey jarvis, what time is it
> hey jarvis, what's the date

It reads the clock from your PC. It has no clock of its own, which is why this
is a tool rather than something it just knows.

## Email

> hey jarvis, anything new in my inbox
> hey jarvis, what came in overnight
> hey jarvis, any unread mail from this week
> hey jarvis, is there anything from Sarah
> hey jarvis, read me the one about the invoice
> hey jarvis, draft a reply saying I'll call them Thursday

Drafts go into your Drafts folder in Gmail for you to check and send. It will
only actually send if you say so and then confirm out loud.

## Files

> hey jarvis, find my files about the Henderson quote
> hey jarvis, read me the notes file
> hey jarvis, make a note called shopping that says eggs, milk, bread

It can only see the one folder set as `WORKSPACE` in settings.txt — Documents
by default. Writing a file asks first.

## Programs and the web

> hey jarvis, open Excel
> hey jarvis, open Chrome
> hey jarvis, look up the weather in Chicago

## Anything else

It is Claude underneath, so ordinary questions work too:

> hey jarvis, how do I get a running total in Excel
> hey jarvis, what's a reasonable way to word a late payment reminder

---

## Things worth knowing

**It keeps replies short on purpose.** Everything it says is read aloud, and a
paragraph that looks fine on screen is a long time to sit through. Ask it to go
into more detail whenever you want more.

**It talks while it is still thinking.** It starts speaking the first sentence
before the rest is finished, which is why it feels quicker than it strictly is.

**It only knows what it looked up.** It is told not to invent messages,
appointments or names. If it did not read something from your mail or your
files, it should say so rather than guess.

**Nothing it heard leaves your PC except what you said to it.** The microphone
is processed on your own machine. Only the resulting text goes to Anthropic to
be answered.
